## Selected projects

### [Disease Outbreak Watch](https://github.com/M-Linet/disease-outbreak-watch) · [Live dashboard](https://m-linet.github.io/disease-outbreak-watch/)
Scans East African news in English and Kiswahili for early signs of disease outbreaks, then extracts the disease, place, and case numbers and flags unusual rises.
- Reached 83.5% cross-validated accuracy on 261 hand-labelled articles
- Picked up Kenya's first confirmed Ebola case on the day it was confirmed when backtested

**Tools:** Python, scikit-learn, pandas, NLP, D3.js

### [Life Expectancy Forecasting](https://github.com/M-Linet/life-expectancy-forecasting) · [Live dashboard](https://life-expectancy-forecasting-3jhtj3bb3hdgrf2psdrj5k.streamlit.app/)
Analyses what drove life expectancy changes across 185 countries from 2000 to 2023 and forecasts the outlook to 2030.
- Backtested forecasts with an average error of 0.73 years, against 1.14 for a no-change baseline
- Estimated how many years meeting the SDG health targets would add

**Tools:** Python, pandas, statsmodels, Plotly, Streamlit

### [Healthcare Access Optimization System](https://github.com/M-Linet/Healthcare-Access-Optimization-System)
A triage prototype that predicts a likely condition from symptoms, matches it to a level of care, and finds a suitable facility among 9,700+ in Kenya.
- Traced a misleading 100% accuracy to duplicated, overly clean training data
- Added rule-based emergency red flags that override the model

**Tools:** Python, scikit-learn, FastAPI, SQLite, Leaflet
